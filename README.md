# ShadowAlpha

AI-powered autonomous trading infrastructure for onchain markets.

## Overview

ShadowAlpha is a modular AI trading system designed for crypto-native operators, combining:

- live market intelligence
- AI-generated trade signals
- risk evaluation
- autonomous execution
- Solana devnet transaction infrastructure

The system is structured into 3 core layers:

1. Intelligence Layer  
   Processes market data, trends, and sentiment.

2. Decision Layer  
   Generates AI trade signals and evaluates risk exposure.

3. Execution Layer  
   Executes onchain transactions through Solana devnet infrastructure.

## AI Signal Engine

ShadowAlpha evaluates market conditions and generates signals such as:

- LONG
- SHORT
- HOLD

Signal generation currently uses:

- price momentum
- volatility detection
- trend scoring
- confidence evaluation

---

## Risk Engine

Built-in protection layer that evaluates:

- volatility exposure
- confidence thresholds
- trade safety conditions

The engine determines whether execution is:

- LOW RISK
- MEDIUM RISK
- HIGH RISK
